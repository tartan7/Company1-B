# F-01 Technical Demo Call Outline
## Prospect: Chuck Herrin (Replacement for Tom Bradford)

## 1) Call Objective
- Confirm Chuck's operational pain points and buying criteria.
- Demonstrate F-01 technical capability against realistic ag staffing scenarios.
- Secure agreement on pilot scope, timeline, and technical integration next step.

## 2) Recommended 30-Minute Agenda
1. Introductions and call goals (2 min)
2. Discovery on current workflow and constraints (5 min)
3. F-01 platform walkthrough (12 min)
4. Technical fit and integration discussion (6 min)
5. Pilot proposal and close (5 min)

## 3) Demo Storyline (What to Show and Why)
- Dashboard overview: establish visibility across farms, crews, and forecast risk.
- Seasonal forecast and demand curve: show 6-8 week labor forecasting logic.
- Skill-based matching: demonstrate assignment quality and gap detection.
- Real-time disruption workflow: simulate weather-driven re-planning and notifications.
- Worker mobile experience: schedule visibility, check-in evidence, and trust/retention.
- Export/report flow: show payroll/compliance handoff readiness.

## 4) Suggested Talk Track
"Chuck, this demo is focused on one thing: reducing manual scheduling load while improving fill rate and worker reliability. I'll show how F-01 predicts labor demand, matches crews by skill and location, and handles same-day disruptions in minutes instead of hours."

"As we go, I want to pressure-test technical fit with your current process so we can decide today whether a pilot is the right next step."

## 5) Discovery Questions (Ask Early)
- How many farms/sites are coordinated at peak season?
- What is current weekly time spent on scheduling and last-minute changes?
- What systems own worker records and payroll today?
- What is the biggest failure mode today: understaffing, no-shows, or compliance/audit gaps?
- What would make a pilot a clear success in 4 weeks?

## 6) Technical Validation Checklist During Call
- Data ingestion path agreed (CSV import/API/manual bootstrap).
- Required worker fields confirmed (skill, language, location, availability, pay class).
- Notification channels confirmed (app push, SMS fallback, email).
- Payroll/accounting export format confirmed.
- Mobile adoption assumptions confirmed (smartphone penetration and fallback path).
- Security/compliance questions captured for follow-up.

## 7) Objections and Responses
- "Forecasts are never accurate."  
  Response: start with historical backtest and track forecast error weekly during pilot.

- "My team won't switch from spreadsheets."  
  Response: run F-01 in parallel with current workflow for pilot weeks 1-2, then reduce manual steps.

- "Workers don't all use smartphones."  
  Response: SMS + web fallback keeps assignment and confirmation coverage.

- "Integration will take too long."  
  Response: phase rollout: bulk import first, deeper integrations after pilot validation.

## 8) Pilot Offer Structure (Proposal)
- Duration: 4 weeks.
- Scope: 5-10 farms, up to agreed worker cap.
- Cadence: weekly 30-min review with operations + technical owner.
- Success metrics:
  - scheduling time reduction (%)
  - fill-rate improvement (%)
  - reassignment time after disruptions (minutes)
  - worker confirmation/check-in rate (%)
- Exit criteria: convert to seasonal rollout plan if KPI targets are met.

## 9) Close Script
"If this technical flow matches your operation, the next step is a 4-week pilot on 5-10 farms. We'll define baseline metrics this week, run weekly reviews, and make a go/no-go decision from measured outcomes. Are you comfortable moving forward on that basis?"
